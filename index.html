FIST · MMU

Bachelor of Information Technology (Hons)
(Security Technology)

Faculty of Information Science and Technology · MQA/FA1235

📄   About the Programme

A future-ready programme designed to equip students at the Faculty of Information Science and Technology (FIST), Multimedia University, with strong technical and professional skills.

◎   Programme Educational Objectives

  • Produce competent IT graduates with strong fundamentals
  • Develop ethical professionals ready for industry
  • Encourage lifelong learning and innovation

🎓   Programme Outcomes

  • Apply knowledge of computing and mathematics
  • Design and develop computing solutions
  • Communicate effectively in a professional context
  • Demonstrate teamwork and leadership

💼   Career Prospects

Security AnalystPenetration TesterSOC AnalystForensic Investigator

📋   Entry Requirements

  • STPM / A-Level: Min CGPA 2.50
  • UEC: Min Grade B in 5 subjects
  • Foundation / Matriculation: Min CGPA 2.50
  • Diploma in related field: Min CGPA 2.50
