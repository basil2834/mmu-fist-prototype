Programme Details

FIST · MMU

Bachelor of Information Technology (Hons) (Security Technology)

Faculty of Information Science and Technology · MQA/FA1235

About the Programme

A future-ready programme designed to equip students with strong technical and professional skills.

Programme Educational Objectives

  • Produce competent IT graduates.
  • Develop ethical professionals.
  • Encourage lifelong learning.

Programme Outcomes

  • Apply knowledge of computing.
  • Design secure computing solutions.
  • Communicate effectively.

Career Prospects

Security AnalystPenetration TesterSOC AnalystForensic Investigator

Entry Requirements

  • STPM / A-Level: Min CGPA 2.50
  • UEC: Min Grade B in 5 subjects
  • Foundation / Diploma: Min CGPA 2.50
